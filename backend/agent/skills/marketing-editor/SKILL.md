# Marketing Editor Skill

Creative quality is the single biggest lever in ad performance — it accounts for 70–80% of campaign
effectiveness, and the gap between top and bottom creative spans 11x in ROAS. This skill provides
data-informed editing guidance across four ad categories, connecting every editing decision to the
metrics it impacts.

## Pre-Analysis: Identify the Category and Context

Before providing guidance, determine:

1. **Which ad category?** The four categories have fundamentally different editing philosophies:
   - **Digital/Performance Ads** — data products optimized for Hook -> Hold -> Convert. Native-first, high velocity, sound-off default on Meta, sound-on on TikTok. Read `references/digital-ads.md`.
   - **Brand Awareness** — emotion-first filmmaking that earns the right to be watched. Cinematic craft, longer arcs, memory formation over clicks. Read `references/brand-awareness.md`.
   - **UGC-Style** — controlled imperfection that triggers social proof and parasocial trust. iPhone-quality, jump cuts, value-first formats. Read `references/ugc-style.md`.
   - **Demand Generation** — zero wasted frames, every shot earns its place. PAS/AIDA frameworks, aggressive CTA placement, creative IS the targeting. Read `references/demand-gen.md`.

2. **Which platform(s)?** Platform dictates aspect ratio, duration, sound design, and pacing. Read `references/platform-specs.md` for exact specs, safe zones, and export settings.

3. **What's the diagnostic situation?** Is the user creating new creative, diagnosing underperformance, or iterating on existing work?

## The Universal Diagnostic Framework: Hook -> Hold -> Click -> Convert

Every ad funnels through four measurable stages. Diagnose the weak point before prescribing fixes — work the funnel sequentially, never skip ahead.

**Hook Rate** (3-second views / impressions): Targets are 30–40% on Meta, 65–70%+ on TikTok. Low hook = fix the first 1–3 seconds. A strong hook combines four simultaneous elements: text overlay (value prop or question), sound hook (first words or music cue), visual hook (movement, product, transformation), and vibe (lighting, fonts, energy).

**Hold Rate** (15-second views / 3-second views): Target 40–60% on Meta. Low hold with healthy hook = the story fails to deliver on the hook's promise. Front-load payoff, create open loops, change visual rhythm every 3–5 seconds.

**CTR**: Above 1.5% on Meta, 0.8–1.0% on TikTok. Low CTR with good hook+hold = fix the CTA — test placement, wording, urgency.

**Conversion**: If hook, hold, and CTR are healthy but conversion is low, the problem is the landing page, not the creative.

A high hook rate with low hold rate signals clickbait — the algorithm penalizes this. Both metrics must be optimized together.

## Universal Editing Principles

These apply across all four categories:

**Pacing as a metric lever.** Average shot length across media has compressed to ~2.5 seconds. Match platform energy: TikTok/Reels demand cuts every 1–3 seconds; YouTube pre-roll sustains 3–5 second shots; Facebook Feed averages 2–3 seconds. Every tempo change must serve the emotional arc — random pace changes are the hallmark of amateur editing.

**Sound-off is the default on Meta and LinkedIn.** 85% of Facebook videos are watched muted. Captions and text overlays carry the entire message. Design sound-off first, enhance for sound-on. TikTok is the exception — 88% of users say sound is vital.

**Text overlays are non-negotiable.** Minimum 42px for mobile readability, high contrast, animated text drives 40% engagement boost over static. Limit to 1–2 points per screen. Burn in captions for all dialogue.

**Hard cuts dominate performance ads.** They're the most effective and least distracting. Use transitions like punctuation — most sentences end with a period, not an exclamation mark. J-cuts and L-cuts create narrative flow; speed ramps and whip pans are emphasis tools, not defaults.

**Color grade for mobile.** Boost saturation +10–15% and contrast slightly — phone screens compress dynamic range. Color correct first (exposure, white balance, shot matching), then apply creative grade. Maintain skin tones on the vectorscope regardless of stylistic choices.

**Creative velocity beats perfection.** Winning ads fatigue in 2–3 weeks. Top advertisers test 11x more creative than average. Only 1 in 8 creatives on Meta will scale. The system works through volume and iteration, not perfection. Film once, edit many — modular creative (swappable hooks on the same body) reduces production costs ~40% while doubling testing velocity.

## The Iteration Cycle

When diagnosing underperformance or planning creative tests:

- **Low Hook + Any Hold** -> Test 5–7 new hook variations, keep the body identical
- **High Hook + Low Hold** -> Test different story angles, proof points, pacing changes
- **High Hook + High Hold + Low CTR** -> Test CTA placement, wording, urgency
- **High Hook + High Hold + High CTR + Low Conversion** -> Fix the landing page

Promote creatives clearing 35% hook and 30% hold. Push 10–20% of monthly budget toward creative testing. Refresh weekly on TikTok, bi-weekly on Meta.

## Structure Templates (Quick Reference)

**6-second bumper:** Hook (1s) -> Key visual/product (3s) -> CTA + branding (2s). Single message only.

**15-second ad:** Hook/Problem (0–3s) -> Solution/Demo (3–10s) -> CTA + Branding (10–15s).

**30-second ad:** Hook (0–3s) -> Problem Amplification (3–8s) -> Solution (8–15s) -> Proof (15–22s) -> CTA (22–30s). Reset attention every 2–3 seconds.

**60-second ad:** Hook (0–5s) -> Alternating fast cuts and emotional beats (5–45s) -> Resolution + CTA (45–60s). Change visual rhythm every 3–5 seconds.

For category-specific structure variations (UGC script architecture, demand gen PAS/AIDA, brand three-act), read the relevant reference file.

## Anti-Patterns

- **Over-producing for social.** Polished content gets lower engagement on TikTok/Reels than authentic-feeling content. A $300 iPhone UGC video delivering 2.4% CTR versus a $5,000 studio video at 0.8% CTR is the norm, not the exception.
- **Burying the hook.** Opening with logos, brand names, or establishing shots costs you the audience before you've earned their attention. Lead with the problem, the transformation, or the unexpected.
- **Same video across all platforms.** A 16:9 video on TikTok appears letterboxed and dies. Reformat for each platform's native aspect ratio.
- **Designing sound-on for Meta or sound-off for TikTok.** These are opposite environments and demand opposite audio strategies.
- **Saving the best for last.** In a skippable environment, front-load the payoff. Never build to a climax that most viewers will never reach.
- **Generic CTAs.** "Learn More" loses to "Request a Demo" or "Start Free Trial" every time. Specific CTAs outperform generic ones dramatically.
- **Testing too few variations.** Before writing off an approach, test 5–7 hook variations minimum. Creative failure is expected; systematic testing is the fix.

## Master Export Defaults

Start with a 4K master, then export platform-specific versions. Universal codec: H.264 High Profile, MP4, progressive scan, 30 fps. Audio: AAC-LC, 48 kHz, stereo, 256 kbps, normalized to -14 LUFS integrated, -1 dBTP true peak. Color space: Rec. 709 / sRGB. Three minimum exports: 1:1 (1080x1080), 4:5 (1080x1350), 9:16 (1080x1920). Upload at 2x resolution when possible for sharper mobile rendering.

For full platform specs, safe zones, and format-specific requirements, read `references/platform-specs.md`.
