# Digital Ads Editing Playbook — Paid Social, Display, and Video Ads

## Editing Philosophy: Data Products, Not Art Projects

Digital ad editing treats pacing, text placement, music, and visual rhythm as core performance levers, not aesthetic preferences. The dominant mental model is Hook -> Hold -> Convert funnel thinking — each zone has distinct editing requirements measured by separate metrics.

**Native-first thinking.** Ads that feel native to the platform get watched; ads that feel inserted get skipped. Premium-looking "upgrades" to social video frequently cause performance declines — the creative looks better, the results are worse.

**Creative velocity over perfection.** Winning ads fatigue in 2–3 weeks. Top advertisers test 11x more creative than average. Maintain a pipeline of fresh variations rather than perfecting a single hero asset.

## Hook Architecture (First 3 Seconds)

65% of people who watch the first 3 seconds will watch for 10+ seconds. 45% will continue past 30 seconds. The hook determines everything.

A strong hook combines four simultaneous elements:
1. **Text overlay hook** — value prop or question visible on screen
2. **Sound hook** — first words spoken or music cue
3. **Visual hook** — movement, product reveal, transformation
4. **Vibe** — lighting, fonts, overall energy setting expectations

### Proven Hook Categories (Tested Across 5,000+ Ads)

- **Investment:** "I spent $500 on 3 different gym memberships before..."
- **Contradiction + Promise:** "Longer Shorts actually get more views, and I'll prove it in 30 seconds"
- **Identity Call-out:** "Hey Product Managers..." — people pay attention to identity confirmation
- **Curiosity/Shock:** "Stop using regular soap" or "The gross thing Reddit convinced me to try"
- **Transformation:** "My skin transformed in 3 days"
- **Authority:** "My friend's mom, who's a dentist, couldn't stop raving about this"
- **POV/Scenario:** "POV: you just discovered..."
- **Reverse Psychology:** Saying something negative about a product — audiences don't expect self-criticism from advertisers

## Structure Templates by Duration

**6-second bumper:**
- Hook (1s) -> Key visual/product (3s) -> CTA + branding (2s)
- Single message, one visual beat, instant brand ID

**15-second ad:**
- Hook/Problem (0–3s) -> Solution/Product Demo (3–10s) -> CTA + Branding (10–15s)

**30-second ad:**
- Hook (0–3s) -> Problem Amplification (3–8s) -> Solution Introduction (8–15s) -> Proof/Social Proof (15–22s) -> CTA (22–30s)
- Reset attention every 2–3 seconds with audio, visuals, or scene changes

**60-second ad:**
- Hook (0–5s) -> Journey with alternating fast/slow beats (5–45s) -> Resolution + CTA (45–60s)
- Change visual rhythm every 3–5 seconds

## Editing Techniques That Move Metrics

### Cut Frequency
- TikTok/Reels: cuts every 1–3 seconds
- YouTube pre-roll: 3–5 second shots with strong content
- Facebook Feed: 2–3 seconds per shot
- Match platform energy; let emotional destination drive pacing choices

### Transitions
- **Hard cuts** dominate — most effective, least distracting
- **Jump cuts** convey urgency and authenticity for vlog/UGC formats
- **Match cuts** for product-to-lifestyle transitions (matching shapes, colors, movements)
- **J-cuts** (next scene's audio before its visual) and **L-cuts** (current audio over next visual) for narrative flow
- Whip pans, zoom transitions, speed ramps: emphasis tools, not defaults

### Color Grading
- Two-step: color correct first (exposure, white balance, shot matching), then creative grade
- For mobile: boost saturation +10–15%, slight contrast increase
- Grade one hero shot first, copy settings across sequence
- Maintain skin tones on vectorscope regardless of stylistic grade
- Warm = trust/invitation; Cool = modern/tech-forward; High contrast = boldness/urgency

### Text and Captions
- 85% of Facebook videos watched muted — text overlays are non-negotiable
- Minimum 42px for mobile readability, high contrast
- Animated text overlays: 40% engagement boost over static
- 1–2 quick points per screen maximum
- Burn in captions for all dialogue and voiceover

### Music and Sound Design
- TikTok: sound-on treatment, trending audio or Commercial Music Library
- Meta: design sound-off first, enhance for sound-on
- Match music beats to cut points for rhythmic editing
- SFX as attention resets every few seconds (whoosh at cuts, notification pings)
- Strategic silence before key messages amplifies impact

## Platform-Specific Editing Shifts

**TikTok:** Native, authentic, fast-paced. 9:16 non-negotiable. Sound-on mandatory. Sweet spot 7–13s for completion, 21–34s for engagement. Change angles every 2–3 seconds.

**Instagram Reels:** Between TikTok and traditional brand content — "authentic but slightly more curated." Trending format adaptation (GRWM, day-in-the-life) performs well. Captions critical due to sound-off viewing.

**YouTube Pre-Roll:** Lives or dies in first 5 seconds before skip button. Supports longer storytelling (30s–3min for consideration). Lean-back, sound-on environment. Non-skippable 15s still needs a strong opening.

**Facebook Feed:** 4:5 vertical preferred (more screen real estate, higher CTR). Tolerates slightly longer story-driven content. Sound-off is default. Optimal 5–15 seconds.

**Google Display:** Clean design, fast loading (<150 KB), text <=20% of image, strong visual hierarchy, clear CTA. Design for clarity at small sizes.

## Mistakes That Kill Performance

1. **Over-producing.** Polished content gets lower engagement than authentic-feeling content on social platforms.
2. **Burying the hook** with logos, brand names, or establishing shots in opening frames.
3. **Same video across all platforms** without format optimization — 16:9 on TikTok = letterboxed death.
4. **Ignoring sound-off on Meta** where most users scroll muted.
5. **Ignoring sound-on for TikTok** where 88% say sound is vital.
6. **Creative stagnation** — not testing enough variations. Only 1 in 8 creatives on Meta will scale; test 5–7 hook variations minimum before writing off an approach.

---

## Modular Creative Approach

Treat ads as interchangeable building blocks: hook (0–3s), body/value prop (3–15s), and CTA (15s+). When a hook fatigues, swap it without rebuilding the entire video. A single shoot can generate 50+ variations by mixing and matching hooks, bodies, and CTAs.

**Film once, edit many.** Start with 4K master, export multiple aspect ratios in one sitting. This modular approach lets editors A/B test hooks without rebuilding entire ads.
