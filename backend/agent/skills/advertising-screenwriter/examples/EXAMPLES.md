# Example output formats

These are format examples, not templates to imitate blindly.

## Example 1: 30s emotional reveal script

User request:
"Write a 30-second holiday ad for a gift brand. Warm, not cheesy."

Response shape:

Concept:
A father seems distracted and absent all month. The reveal: he has been secretly learning sign language to surprise his daughter at the school play.

Why it works:
It uses a misread behavior as the setup, then reveals love through effort rather than sentiment. The brand lands as a tool for meaningful giving, not as a hard sell.

Script:
[0:00-0:06]
We see a father half-listening at dinner. Missing jokes. Looking down at his phone.
The daughter notices.

[0:06-0:13]
On the train: phone in hand.
At work: phone in hand.
In bed: phone in hand.
The daughter's face hardens. He's somewhere else.

[0:13-0:22]
School auditorium.
Children step onstage.
The daughter looks nervous.

Music drops.

From the crowd, the father raises his hands.
He signs: "You've got this."

Her whole face changes.

[0:22-0:27]
She performs. Confident now.
From the wings, her mother sees him still signing every word.

[0:27-0:30]
SUPER: The best gifts say what words can't.

## Example 2: 15s comic script

User request:
"Write a funny 15-second snack ad based on stress eating."

Response shape:

Concept:
A guy tries to act calm in chaos, but his snack choice reveals the truth.

Script:
[Office. Total disaster.]
Someone whispers: "You good?"
He straightens his shirt. "Never better."

He opens his desk drawer.

It is filled wall-to-wall with the same snack.

Beat.

VO: Everyone's got a plan.
Some are just crunchier than others.

LOGO.

## Example 3: audio/visual format

User request:
"Write a premium tech brand film. Minimal dialogue."

Response shape:

| VISUAL | AUDIO |

| Dark apartment before dawn. A woman laces her shoes. City still asleep. | Soft room tone. No music yet. |

## Example 4: script diagnosis + rewrite

User draft:
"We believe in innovation, connection, and empowering modern lifestyles."

Useful response shape:
- What is weak
- What truth is missing
- What dramatic angle would fix it
- A rewritten version

Example:
What's weak:
This is abstract category language. There is no human truth, no scene, no tension, and no reason for the audience to care.

Better angle:
Show the tiny real moment where connection matters and almost fails.

Rewrite:
A mother outside a locked school gate.
Phone at 1%.
Rain starting.
One tap.
The door buzzes.
Her son runs out, furious for half a second, then relieved.
VO: "The moments that matter never arrive on schedule."
LOGO.
